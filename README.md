# 🎮 YOU ARE THE NPC

> **You're not the hero. You're the person living in the hero's world.**

**YOU ARE THE NPC** is an open-world simulation / mystery game built in **C++** where you play as an ordinary NPC living in a world controlled by someone else.

You have a job.  
You have a home.  
You have friends.  
You have a routine.

And then you notice something that doesn't belong.

Someone in your city isn't following the rules.

---

## 🧠 The Concept

Most games make you the protagonist.

**This game doesn't.**

You are one of thousands of people living in a simulated city. Other characters have their own schedules, relationships, jobs, memories, and goals.

Somewhere in the city, however, there is a **player-controlled character**.

They're the hero of the story.

You're not.

You simply have to live in the world they've created.

---

## 🏙️ A Living City

The city continues to exist whether you're watching it or not.

NPCs can:

- 🚶 Walk around the city
- 💼 Go to work
- 🍔 Visit restaurants
- 🏠 Return home
- 🛒 Go shopping
- 👥 Meet friends
- ❤️ Build relationships
- 🚗 Drive around
- 🚨 React to crimes and emergencies
- 🗣️ Talk about things they've witnessed

Every NPC has their own routine.

---

## 👤 The Player

Eventually, you notice someone behaving strangely.

They don't follow normal schedules.

They enter restricted areas.

They steal cars.

They get into fights.

They disappear from places they shouldn't be able to leave.

And nobody seems to question it.

You begin to suspect that this person isn't just another NPC.

**They're being controlled.**

---

## 🔎 Investigate the World

There are no giant quest markers telling you what to do.

Instead, you discover the story by **observing the world**.

You might notice that:

> A coworker suddenly disappears.

You ask around.

Someone says:

> "They quit yesterday."

But you remember seeing them leave work.

Something isn't right.

Follow clues.

Talk to people.

Watch suspicious characters.

Investigate strange events.

Piece together what is actually happening.

---

## 🧠 NPC Memory

NPCs don't simply forget everything that happens.

They can remember important events.

For example:

```text
NPC #184

Memory:
- Saw the player steal a red car
- Saw the player near the bank
- Knows John disappeared
- Saw police investigating the area

Beliefs:
- The player is dangerous
- Something suspicious is happening

Relationships:
- John → Friend
- Sarah → Neutral
- Player → Suspicious
```

What NPCs remember can affect how they behave later.

---

## ⚠️ Your Choices Matter

You can decide how involved you want to become.

You could:

- Ignore everything
- Investigate quietly
- Warn other NPCs
- Follow the player
- Report suspicious behavior
- Help the player
- Sabotage their plans
- Become their enemy

There isn't always a "correct" choice.

---

## 🔥 Dynamic Events

The world can generate unexpected situations.

Examples:

- 🚗 Car accidents
- 🚨 Police investigations
- 🏦 Robberies
- 🏥 Missing people
- 🔥 Fires
- 👊 Street fights
- 🚔 Police chases
- 🕵️ Suspicious activity
- 👤 NPC disappearances

These events can affect NPC schedules, relationships, and future events.

---

## 💀 Multiple Endings

Your actions can lead to different outcomes.

Possible endings include:

### **NORMAL LIFE**
You never discover what is really happening.

### **THE INVESTIGATOR**
You uncover the truth behind the mysterious player.

### **THE HUNTER**
You begin actively tracking the player and interfering with their plans.

### **THE HERO**
The NPCs begin seeing **you** as the person capable of changing the city.

### **ESCAPE**
You discover something disturbing about the world itself.

### **RESET**
The player's story reaches its conclusion.

Everything resets.

Everyone forgets.

**Except you.**

---

## 🎯 Main Goal

The goal isn't simply to complete missions.

It's to create a world where you can genuinely wonder:

> **"What is happening when I'm not looking?"**

The city shouldn't feel like a collection of NPCs waiting for the player.

It should feel like a world that **exists without you.**
